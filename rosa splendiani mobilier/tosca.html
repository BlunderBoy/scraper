Modular sofa Tosca

The modular outdoor sofa "Tosca" by Rosa Splendiani , embodies the evolution of modularity for furniture outdoor.

 

It is possible to set up the most suitable composition for different space and furnishing requirements. There are six modular elements: a single module, a classic corner module, a large corner module, a double module and a pouff module. In addition, there are pouff-stools in various diameters and heights.

 

The structure is made of QuickDry technology draining rubber (breathable, prevents the formation of mould and microbes) and MultiTech marine multilayer, of certified quality.

The upholstery of the sofa Tosca is made of a special fabric for outdoor use with very high standards of light resistance (prevents the sun's rays from discolouring the fabric over time). Naturally, the fabrics used are water-repellent and water-resistant. They also have ahigh resistance to abrasion, rubbing and staining.

 

The outdoor sofa Tosca is characterised by its minimalist design and straight lines, as well as by the infinite possibilities for customising the fabrics.

You can customise the fabrics as you wish, choosing from our fabric folders.

 

Elements of the collection Tosca:

  • 80-100 Single element (dimensions 70 x 90 x 42/72 h)
  • 80-101 Classic corner element (dimensions 90 x 90 x 42/72 h)
  • 80-102 Double element (dimensions 140 x 90 x 42/72 h)
  • 80-103 Corner element large (dimensions 115 x 115 x 42/72 h)
  • 80-104 Pouff element (dimensions 70 x 90 x 42/72 h)
  • 80-105 Round pouff diam.80 (size diameter 80, h 45)
  • 80-105 Round pouff diam.50 (size diameter 50, h 55)

 

MATERIALS/COMPOSITION :
Structure: Certified marine plywood + Elastic straps
Padding: QuickDry Technologydraining rubber + Dacron
Feet: Aluminium
Upholstery: Water-repellent, photoresistant, stain-resistant Dralon/Acrylic/PVC fabrics
