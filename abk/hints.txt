Each collection on abk.it (or moooiceramicsurfaces.com for Moooi) maps to one product on our website.
Each color/shade listed in links.csv under that collection becomes a variant on that product.

Moooi products (moooiceramicsurfaces.com) follow the same pattern but use manufacturer="MOOOI BY ABK".

links.csv has one URL per collection (Link variante on the first row of each block); all colors in that block are scraped from that page.
Product-level fields (finishes, sizes, thickness) are merged/unioned across all colors in the same collection.
