Casalgrande Padana (casalgrandepadana.com) — one product per collection, one variant per color/shade.

Each collection page on the site maps to a single product on our website. Colors, decori, and special pieces within that collection are variants (variant color = the CSV "Nume produs" shade name).

The site is a Next.js SPA. Product data is embedded in the #__NEXT_DATA__ script tag as props.pageProps.prodotto (titolo, descrizione HTML, mainImage, detailImages, documenti, attributi with colori/decori/pezziSpeciali, formatiSpecifici, superfici, spessoreSpecifico).

Product fields (description, sizes, finishes, thickness, material) come from the collection JSON, merged across all shades in the collection. Variant galleries use the matched colori/decori/pezziSpeciali swatch image plus collection main/detail images.

PDFs are per-collection (catalogs, technical features, declarations of performance) — linked once per product, not per variant.
